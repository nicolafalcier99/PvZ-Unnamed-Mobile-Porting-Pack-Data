# Unnamed's 26 mod-only plants: the Almanac's "More plants" pages and the short hints on the lawn (feature modAlmanac,
# a port addition: the mod itself has no Almanac pages for them). Edit freely. Format: a [KEY] line, then the text.
# Numbers follow what the port does (rules in data/rules-mod-plants.json, evidence in SOURCES-mod-plants.md).
#   MOD_PLANT_0x.._DESCRIPTION   Almanac page text, with the game's colour codes: {KEYWORD} brown, {STAT} red,
#                                {NORMAL} blue-grey, {SHORTLINE} a small gap. ({FLAVOR} joins lines into one paragraph.)
#   MOD_PLANT_0x.._HOW_TO_USE    Almanac page, one line
#   MOD_HINT_PLANTED_0x..        lawn hint, once per level, when you plant it
#   MOD_HINT_SHADOW_0x..         lawn hint instead, when it is planted without enough Moonflowers for its shadow state
#   MOD_HINT_TAP_0x..            lawn hint when you tap it and it cannot act
#   MOD_HINT_REFUSED_0x..        lawn hint instead of the Coffee Bean message when it cannot be planted there
# Delete a hint's text (or set modAlmanac.hints to 0 in data/rules-mod-almanac.json) to switch it off.

[MOD_ALMANAC_MORE_PLANTS]
MORE PLANTS

[MOD_ALMANAC_BACK]
FIRST PLANTS

[MOD_ALMANAC_HOW_TO_USE]
How to use:

[MOD_ALMANAC_SECONDS]
s

[MOD_PLANT_0x50_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 20, slows
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Shoots a slowing pea every 2 seconds.
In shadow state it fires a shadow beam instead: 30 damage to every zombie ahead in its row, and slows them.

[MOD_PLANT_0x50_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant a Moonflower next to it (diagonals count) to give it shadow state.

[MOD_PLANT_0x51_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Sun:{STAT} 40 every 24 s
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Drops a sun and a small sun about every 24 seconds.
In shadow state it drops 65 sun, and 1 time in 5 a free sun-plant card instead of the big sun.

[MOD_PLANT_0x51_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it early like a Sunflower; a Moonflower next to it makes it give more.

[MOD_PLANT_0x52_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} huge, then 250 per shard
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Blows up like a Cherry Bomb, then throws 6 shards up, down and on the diagonals (250 damage to the first zombie each one hits).
A spinning core stays behind: it hits the first zombie that walks into it, or becomes a Cherry Bomb 14 seconds later.

[MOD_PLANT_0x52_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it in the middle of a group of zombies, like a Cherry Bomb.

[MOD_PLANT_0x53_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 1800 per hit
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Rolls across the lawn like a bowling nut, on any level, bouncing between rows and knocking zombies down (less against helmets and shields).
Zombies never eat it.

[MOD_PLANT_0x53_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it near the left end of a row with zombies: it starts rolling at once.

[MOD_PLANT_0x55_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 20 per ice pea
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Sprays 500 ice peas in a wide fan for 5 seconds, then vanishes.
While it stands, every pea on the lawn becomes an ice pea.
When it vanishes, all your Peashooters turn into Snow Peas.

[MOD_PLANT_0x55_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it when many zombies come at once; plant Peashooters first if you want them upgraded.

[MOD_PLANT_0x56_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 100 + health/15 (max 600)
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Burns every zombie from just behind it to 3 squares ahead, in 3 rows. The fire thaws frozen and slowed zombies.
In shadow state it also weakens them (double damage for 4 s).

[MOD_PLANT_0x56_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it in a middle row; its fire undoes your ice plants' slowing.

[MOD_PLANT_0x57_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 20 per pea
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Shoots 2 peas at a time.
1 pea in 10 knocks the zombie back (40 damage); 1 in 10 poisons it (15 damage, then 500 more over 5 seconds).

[MOD_PLANT_0x57_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it like a Repeater.

[MOD_PLANT_0x58_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Poison:{STAT} 20 every 0.64 s
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Does not attack. A zombie that eats it is poisoned for the rest of the level.
In shadow state the poison skips helmets and shields and hurts the zombie itself.

[MOD_PLANT_0x58_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it where zombies will eat it; a Moonflower next to it makes the poison skip armour.

[MOD_PLANT_0x59_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Sun:{STAT} 150 at once
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Costs no sun. Drops 3 big suns in one and a half seconds, then vanishes.
Its card starts each level recharging.

[MOD_PLANT_0x59_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it on any free square as soon as it is ready.

[MOD_PLANT_0x5b_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Gives:{STAT} a free seed card
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Goes on top of one of your plants. A few seconds later it eats that plant and drops the plant's seed card.
Tap the card to plant it again for free.

[MOD_PLANT_0x5b_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it on top of any of your plants; a few seconds later it eats that plant and drops its card for free.

[MOD_PLANT_0x5c_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Effect:{STAT} the eater becomes an Imp
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}The zombie that finishes eating it turns into an Imp with no armour and at most 270 health. The Imp still fights you.
Bosses are not changed.

[MOD_PLANT_0x5c_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it in front of big zombies such as Gargantuars or Football Zombies.

[MOD_PLANT_0x5d_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 300 to 550 per shot
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Never shoots by itself. Each tap costs 50 sun and fires a prism beam along its row.
The beam does 300 damage, plus 1 for every 4 sun you still have (up to 550).

[MOD_PLANT_0x5d_HOW_TO_USE]
Tap it to fire when a strong zombie is in its row; more sun saved means a stronger shot.

[MOD_PLANT_0x67_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Toughness:{STAT} 8000
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}When zombies have bitten 1500 health off it, then every 1200 more, a hypnotised Newspaper, Football Zombie or Gargantuar rises to fight for you.
In shadow state it heals, hurts biters and calls a Gargantuar.

[MOD_PLANT_0x67_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it at the front; 2 Moonflowers next to it (a shadowed one counts 2) give it shadow state.

[MOD_PLANT_0x69_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Sun:{STAT} 15 every 24 s
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Gives shadow state to the shadow plants in the 8 squares around it: Shadow Peashooter, Primal Sunflower, Toxic Shadow-shroom, Fire-dragon Grass, Charmed Tall-nut, Dream Starfruit, Dusk-pult.
With 3 shadow plants around it, it is shadowed too: 25 sun, and it counts as 2.

[MOD_PLANT_0x69_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it in the middle of your shadow plants.

[MOD_PLANT_0x6b_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Effect:{STAT} once, the whole lawn
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Half a second after you plant it, every zombie on the lawn goes back to the right edge, but all of them are healed to full health.
Bosses are not affected. Zombies ignore it.

[MOD_PLANT_0x6b_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it when zombies get close to your house; it works once.

[MOD_PLANT_0x6d_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 17 to 27 per star
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Shoots 5 stars in changing colours. Blue may freeze. Red pierces 5 and may knock off helmets. Purple may hypnotise. Grey pierces 3 and weakens. Green may blow zombies away.
In shadow state its stars chase zombies.

[MOD_PLANT_0x6d_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it mid-lawn; 3 Moonflowers next to it (a shadowed one counts 2) give it shadow state.

[MOD_PLANT_0x6e_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Toughness:{STAT} 4000
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}A Pumpkin that stops the Zomboni from freezing the plant inside it.
It cannot thaw a plant that is already frozen.

[MOD_PLANT_0x6e_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it over a plant in a row where a Zomboni will drive.

[MOD_PLANT_0x70_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 40, splash 13
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Lobs dusk cabbages. Each one splashes 13 damage on the zombies around it in 3 rows, pushes them back a little and weakens them for 2 seconds (double damage).
In shadow state it also lobs at the rows above and below.

[MOD_PLANT_0x70_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it behind your wall; a Moonflower next to it lets it hit 3 rows.

[MOD_PLANT_0x72_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 40
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Lobs butter every time. Each hit holds the zombie in place for 4 more seconds.

[MOD_PLANT_0x72_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it behind your wall to keep zombies stuck in front of it.

[MOD_PLANT_0x73_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Effect:{STAT} wakes the 3x3 around it
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Goes on top of any plant. Wakes up every sleeping plant in the 8 squares around it and its own, then crumbles and heals the plant under it by 30% of its health (up to 300).

[MOD_PLANT_0x73_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it on a plant in the middle of a group of sleeping mushrooms.

[MOD_PLANT_0x75_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Effect:{STAT} bounces zombie shots
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Zombie shots (peas, basketballs, vases) flying at it in its row and the rows above and below bounce back at the zombies.
Each bounce costs it a little health.

[MOD_PLANT_0x75_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it in front of the plants you want to keep safe from zombies that shoot or throw.

[MOD_PLANT_0x76_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Gives:{STAT} a seed card every 24 s
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}About every 24 seconds it drops a random seed card: any plant except the Loaded Cannon.
Tap the card to plant it for free. It stops after the last wave, like a Marigold.

[MOD_PLANT_0x76_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it early in a safe square and pick up the cards it drops.

[MOD_PLANT_0x78_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 500 every 5 s
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Every 5 seconds it hits the toughest zombie to its right, in any row, for 500 damage. No bullet flies: a red crosshair marks its target.
It never picks a boss.

[MOD_PLANT_0x78_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it anywhere; it aims by itself.

[MOD_PLANT_0x7a_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} 12 to 56 per puff
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Fumes around itself like a Gloom-shroom. It takes fire or ice after 2 s, then every 8 s; its last 3 elements decide the attack. 3 fire: 56 damage, sometimes knocks off armour. 3 ice: slows, sometimes freezes. 2 fire + 1 ice: 12 damage in a 5x5 area. 2 ice + 1 fire: heals itself.

[MOD_PLANT_0x7a_HOW_TO_USE]
Tap it to switch the element it takes next (blue means ice).

[MOD_PLANT_0x7b_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} varies
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}Fires a random homing shot at any zombie on the lawn: pea, snow pea, cabbage, melon, star, cob, butter, prism beam and more.

[MOD_PLANT_0x7b_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it on any free square; it finds targets on the whole lawn.

[MOD_PLANT_0x7f_DESCRIPTION]
{KEYWORD}Damage:{STAT} up to 3000 (cob), 3600 (blast)
{SHORTLINE}
{KEYWORD}A Cob Cannon that never reloads by itself. A Kernel-pult card loads a strong cob; a Doom-shroom card sets off a lawn-wide blast at once (no crater, clears every grave). The card used recharges 60 s longer.

[MOD_PLANT_0x7f_HOW_TO_USE]
Plant it on two free squares; drop a Kernel-pult card on it, tap it, then tap the target. Reload after every shot.
